Welcome! Flappy AA Pearl is a lightweight, reflex‑sharpening arcade game. Tap, fly, and beat your high score — no distractions, just pure fun.

🎯 How to play

Tap / click anywhere to make the bird flap. Dodge the pipes, stay alive, and earn points. It’s simple, but mastering it takes practice.
